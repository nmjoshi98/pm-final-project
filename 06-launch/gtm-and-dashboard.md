# GTM Strategy & Success Dashboard

># GTM Launch Plan, RouteLogic (B2B)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | One-Click Compliance Checklist |
| Goal | Awareness |
| Launch tier | L, Multi-channel |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Awareness, Engagement. This feature improves an existing, high-friction workflow for current RouteLogic Velocity coordinators. The launch should focus on driving adoption of the One-Click Compliance Checklist so coordinators complete compliance work faster within Velocity, with less manual effort and no decline in compliance accuracy.
- **Target audience:** Existing RouteLogic Velocity Frontline Dispatchers / Logistics Coordinators who regularly complete compliance checklists as part of daily fleet operations.

## Launch Tier
- **L, Multi-channel**, M, Targeted launch. Focus communications on existing RouteLogic Velocity coordinators and customer teams who use the compliance workflow. The feature can improve daily productivity and strengthen product adoption and retention, but its specialized B2B reach does not justify a broad market campaign. Without targeted communication and enablement, coordinators may continue using the existing manual process and the product may fail to realize the expected adoption benefit.

## Channels
1. **Owned: In-app product messaging, Owned  Use a launch banner or contextual prompt directly in RouteLogic Velocity’s compliance workflow so coordinators encounter the new One-Click Compliance Checklist at the moment they need it.**
2. **Earned: Customer champion / peer advocacy, Earned**
3. **Paid: Targeted LinkedIn Sponsored Content, Paid**

## Enablement & Assets
Create a one-page quick-start guide, a 2-minute demo video, and in-app guidance showing the new One-Click Compliance Checklist workflow. Provide Customer Success and Support with launch talking points, FAQs, troubleshooting guidance, and escalation steps before launch. Include a short customer-facing email template focused on faster compliance completion without compromising accuracy.

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** PM: Owns GTM plan, launch readiness, adoption metrics, and post-launch review. Designer: Owns quick-start guide, in-app messaging, and 2-minute demo. Engineering: Owns production readiness, instrumentation, and launch support. Customer Success Lead: Owns account outreach, customer champion enablement, FAQs, and feedback collection. Support: Receives product briefing and escalation guide before launch. Marketing: Supports targeted LinkedIn Sponsored Content if used. Keep spend minimal for this targeted B2B launch, prioritizing existing team capacity and owned channels over paid media.
- **Timeline:** Phase 1, Weeks 1–2: Prepare & enable. Finalize production readiness, instrumentation, quick-start guide, demo, FAQs, CS/Support training, and identify customer champions. Phase 2, Week 3: Launch. Activate in-app messaging, targeted customer communications, and customer champion outreach; monitor adoption, completion time, and compliance guardrails. Phase 3, Week 4+: Optimize. Review usage and support feedback, measure compliance completion time against the 14.6-minute baseline, monitor compliance accuracy, and decide whether to iterate or expand the launch.

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** Feature adoption: % of eligible coordinators who use the One-Click Compliance Checklist within the first 30 days.
 2. Workflow efficiency: Reduce average compliance completion time from 14.6 minutes to ≤11.7 minutes (≥20% improvement).
 3. Quality guardrail: Compliance accuracy/completeness does not decline, with no increase in incomplete or invalid submissions.
- **Bad signal to watch for:** Bad signal to watch for  High feature exposure but low repeat usage, or faster completion accompanied by increased compliance errors. This would indicate that the workflow appears easier but is not delivering sustainable or safe adoption.
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Most likely post-launch decision  Double down if adoption grows and completion time improves by ≥20% without harming compliance accuracy. Iterate if adoption is healthy but time savings are below target. Roll back or deprioritize if usage remains low or compliance errors increase.
